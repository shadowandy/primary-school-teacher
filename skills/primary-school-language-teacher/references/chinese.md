# Chinese Language 华文 — Primary 1 to 6

Source: 《小学华文课程标准2024》 — MOE Chinese Language Syllabus (Primary), 2024 P1 cohort (ISBN 978-981-18-7820-6) — together with the two 《欢乐伙伴》 character lists, for 华文 and for 高级华文. None of those PDFs ship with the skill: everything needed to fix a course and level, teach, set, mark and look up is in this file and in [`chinese-characters.json`](chinese-characters.json). The page numbers below record where a claim came from in the source document, for anyone auditing it; they are not somewhere to go and read.

Throughout, **CL** = 华文, **HCL** = 高级华文, **FCL** = 基础华文.

## Stages and courses

Two stages: **奠基阶段** (foundation-building, P1-P4) — itself split into 第一阶段 (P1-P2) and 第二阶段 (P3-P4) — and **定向阶段** (orientation, P5-P6).

Three courses, by ability:

| Course | Levels | For |
|---|---|---|
| **华文** (CL) | P1-P6 | middle ability — the mainstream course |
| **高级华文** (HCL) | P1-P6 | stronger ability. P1-P2 HCL runs only in SAP schools (特选小学) |
| **基础华文** (Foundation CL) | **P5-P6 only** | weaker ability |

Weekly periods: 华文 6h at P1-2, 4.5h P3, 4h P4, 4.5h P5-6. 高级华文 7h at P1-2, 5.5h P3, 5h P4, 5.5h P5-6. 基础华文 2.5h at P5-6.

## Unit model — and why it sets the exam ceiling

Each course is built from **单元** (units), not a flat syllabus:

| Unit | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **核心单元** core | Compulsory for every student. Guarantees a common set of learning objectives and sets the floor for Chinese attainment. **70-80% of curriculum time.** |
| **导入单元** bridging | For lower-primary students with little prior exposure to Chinese, to prepare them for the core units. Taught *before* the core unit. |
| **强化单元** reinforcement | For middle-primary students needing extra help; reinforces prior content to prepare for core units. Delivered under the ABLE《华文我也行》mother tongue support programme. Taught *before* the core unit. |
| **校本单元** school-based | For middle-ability students; designed by the school to its own students' needs. Taught *after* the core unit. |
| **深广单元** enrichment | For stronger students with an interest in Chinese. Taught *after* the core unit. |

Unit combinations — 华文: core + (bridging/reinforcement **or** school-based **or** enrichment). 高级华文: core + (school-based **or** enrichment). 基础华文: core + school-based. In every course the core unit takes 70-80% of curriculum time and the chosen second unit the remaining 20-30%.

A school builds its 校本单元 in any of three ways the syllabus names (p18): take parts of the bridging/reinforcement or enrichment units to sharpen targeting, use the core unit itself but enrich the activities around it, or write its own material. So "school-based" is not a fixed body of content, and a user's 校本单元 material is theirs to describe — ask rather than assume.

P1 is treated loosely on purpose: because P1 children are just starting out and their performance is unstable, schools may handle P1 unit-streaming flexibly to suit their own needs (p17).

**The PSLE Chinese paper is pitched to the level of the 核心单元.** This is the single most load-bearing fact for setting practice: enrichment-unit material is above the exam's pitch, and a child struggling with core units should not be set 深广单元 work.

## Learning focus by stage

| Course | Stage | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 华文 | 奠基 (P1-P4) | Build basic reading and writing (写话, 写段); emphasise 聆听, 说话, 口语互动, 识字 and 写字 |
| 华文 | 定向 (P5-P6) | Integrate all skills; emphasise **口语互动**, 阅读, 写作, 书面互动 |
| 高级华文 | 奠基 (P1-P4) | Same as 华文 奠基 |
| 高级华文 | 定向 (P5-P6) | Integrate all skills; emphasise 阅读, 写作, 书面互动 (note: **not** 口语互动) |
| 基础华文 | 定向 (P5-P6) | Build basic reading and writing; emphasise 聆听, 说话 and 口语互动 |

# Objectives 分项目标

Objectives are set along three axes — **语言能力** (language ability), **人文素养** (humanities/cultural literacy) and **通用能力** (general competencies) — and span six language skills:

| Input 语言输入 | Output 语言输出 | Interaction 语言互动 |
|---|---|---|
| 聆听 listening · 阅读 reading | 说话 speaking · 写作 writing | 口语互动 spoken interaction · 书面互动 written interaction |

The six are taught in an integrated way, not in isolation. Courses differ along three dimensions: **the number of objectives** (the courses require different 识字量, for instance), **the pace at which they start** (self- and peer-assessment of writing begins at P5 in 华文 but P4 in 高级华文), and **the depth of content** (华文 and 高级华文 may use the same subject matter with differing richness and complexity of language). The stage objectives were themselves derived from MOE's 语言能力描述, a separate set of proficiency descriptors running P1 to JC2 across all six skills, which is why the levels spiral rather than restart.

Each objective below carries the grade span over which it runs, as **[CL 1-2 · HCL 1-2 · FCL 5-6]** — the grades at which a course *begins and completes* that objective. **A course not named does not carry that objective at all**, and that is how to answer "is X in or out" for 基础华文: FCL is P5-P6 only, and it carries a deliberately reduced set.

Within a span the objective does not stand still. The syllabus's own words: the later stage demands more than the earlier one in the depth and breadth of the material, in familiarity with the knowledge and in fluency of the skill. So `[CL 1-6]` means taught from P1 and still being deepened at P6, not mastered at P1.

> **A limit of this reference.** These spans were recovered from the colour shading in the syllabus's own tables (Chapter 4, 分项目标) by two independent methods that agree, and the recovered span for the one objective whose pacing the syllabus also states in prose — 自评和互评, CL from P5 and HCL from P4 — matches that prose. They are reliable. What is not here is the syllabus's 语言能力描述, the level-by-level proficiency descriptors the objectives were graded against; that document is a separate publication and no part of it is in this file. When a user needs that depth of precision, say that this reference does not carry it and work from the user's own material — their textbook, their school's scheme of work — which is in any case the better evidence about the cohort in front of them.

## 聆听 Listening

*Text types:* 口头指示, 口头报告, 对话, 广告, 新闻报道, 儿歌, 歌曲, 故事, 广播, 电影, 电视, 网络音频及视频.

*Methods and habits:* listen attentively, patiently and with focus; learn and accumulate language through listening; think while listening. *Attitudes:* enjoy listening to Mandarin; actively broaden listening exposure.

*Knowledge and skills (p25):*

1. **Recognise the phonetic features of Mandarin**
   - distinguish 音节, 声母, 韵母, 声调 — [CL 1-2 · HCL 1-2]
   - appreciate different 语气 (statement, question, exclamation) and 语调 — [CL 3-6 · HCL 3-6 · FCL 5-6]
2. **Understand spoken material**
   - use visual and auditory cues (gesture, expression, tone, intonation) — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - understand specific information (person, time, place, cause, course, outcome) — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - grasp main content and extract key information — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - recognise common text types (advertisement, news, story) — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - infer implied information — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]
3. **Analyse spoken material**
   - sequence events — [CL 3-6 · HCL 3-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - distinguish different viewpoints — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6]
   - infer and predict from context and key words — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]

## 说话 Speaking

*Text types:* 见闻, 故事, 感想, 展示与讲述. *Methods:* accumulate material from daily life to enrich content; think carefully and take responsibility for one's words. *Attitudes:* willing, proactive, confident in expressing oneself in Mandarin.

*Knowledge and skills (p26):*

1. **Master pronunciation**
   - read 声母, 韵母, 声调, 音节 clearly and correctly — [CL 1-2 · HCL 1-2]
   - accurate pronunciation, clear articulation — [CL 1-4 · HCL 1-4 · FCL 5-6]
2. **Plan content and organise speech**
   - draw on life experience and prior knowledge — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - use aids (objects, pictures) or media (slides) — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - recount what was seen and heard, expressing feelings and views — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - retell what was heard or read — [CL 3-6 · HCL 3-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - speak on topic in an organised way — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - give simple analysis with explanation — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6 · FCL 5-6]
3. **Speak accurately and fluently**
   - appropriate volume and pace — [CL 2-6 · HCL 2-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - clear and complete expression — [CL 3-6 · **HCL 2-6** · FCL 5-6]
   - appropriate 语气 and 语调 — [CL 3-6 · HCL 3-6 · FCL 5-6]

## 口语互动 Spoken interaction

*Types:* 问候, 请求, 邀请, 道歉, 询问, 告知, 劝说, 建议, 称赞, 拒绝, 交谈, 讨论, 采访. *Methods:* interact naturally, graciously and politely; consider others' feelings and respect their opinions; learn and accumulate language through conversation; correct and improve one's own expression through it.

*Attitudes:* willing, and then proactive and confident, in using Mandarin with others.

*Knowledge and skills (p27):*

1. **Interact purposefully**
   - raise questions or requests and respond appropriately to others' — [CL 1-4 · HCL 1-4 · FCL 5-6]
   - quote and paraphrase others' words — [CL 3-5 · HCL 3-5 · FCL 5-6]
   - offer views or suggestions and respond appropriately to others' — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - choose content and wording to suit audience and situation — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6]
2. **Interact using appropriate means**
   - use expression and gesture to support meaning — [CL 1-4 · HCL 1-4 · FCL 5-6]
   - manage effective turn-taking — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - complete tasks through collaborative discussion — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - adjust content or wording in response to the other party's reaction — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]

## 阅读 Reading

*Material types:* children's works (儿歌, 故事, 童话, 寓言), everyday texts (通知, 海报, 广告, 菜单), media (报章, 杂志, 网页). *Methods:* seek out reading material actively and read to a plan; learn language and build knowledge through reading; use appropriate methods, tools and resources; read with focus, thinking while reading. *Attitudes:* enjoy learning characters and want to learn them independently; love and care for Chinese books; enjoy reading and share books with others.

*Knowledge and skills (p28-29):*

1. **Recognise 字形, 字音, 字义** — the character-count bands below
   - recognise 偏旁 and 部首 — [CL 1-2 · HCL 1-2]
2. **Understand reading material**
   - use punctuation to aid understanding — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - use 汉语拼音, pictures, images and reference tools — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - read aloud suitable passages correctly, fluently and with feeling — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - locate specific information in the text — [CL 1-4 · HCL 1-4 · FCL 5-6]
   - recognise common material types (advertisement, news, story) — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - understand main content or key information (中心, 主题) — [CL 5-6 · **HCL 4-6**]
   - understand implied information — [CL 5-6 · **HCL 4-6**]
3. **Analyse, evaluate and apply**
   - predict content from title or pictures — [CL 3-6 · HCL 3-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - infer the meaning of words and sentences — [CL 4-6 · **HCL 3-6**]
   - predict developments or outcomes from cause and course — [CL 5-6 · **HCL 4-6**]
   - compare (characters' words and deeds, changes in things) — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]
   - summarise (paragraph gist, character traits, theme) — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]
   - give views on content (characters, events) — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]
   - apply knowledge and information from texts in life and learning — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]
4. **Use appropriate methods, tools or resources to support reading** — [CL 1-4 · HCL 1-4 · FCL 5-6]

The whole of objective 3 is outside 基础华文 except predicting content from the title or pictures. An FCL comprehension paper therefore asks a child to locate, recognise and predict-from-title; inference, comparison, summary and opinion questions are above that course, and 理解主要内容/隐含信息 are above it too. In 华文, inference and summary arrive together at P5 — which is why P5 is the year comprehension work changes character.

## 写作 Writing

*Material types:* 故事, 日记, 见闻, 感受. *Methods:* correct pen grip and posture; neat, upright, tidy handwriting; observe life and think actively to accumulate material. *Attitudes:* enjoy writing characters; willing to express in writing; enjoy writing in Chinese.

*Knowledge and skills (p30-31):*

1. **Write characters correctly** — the character-count bands below
   - master 基本笔画 and write by 笔顺 rules — [CL 1-3 · HCL 1-3]
   - attend to 间架结构 (character proportion and structure) — [CL 1-3 · HCL 1-3]
2. **Use 汉语拼音 for input** of characters, words and sentences — [CL 1-4 · HCL 1-4 · FCL 5-6]
3. **Plan and organise content**
   - observe closely and use imagination — [CL 2-6 · HCL 2-6]
   - arrange paragraphs and structure a whole piece (组织成篇) — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6]
   - select suitable material from life and reading experience — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]
4. **Express correctly and fluently**
   - use punctuation correctly — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - write complete sentences — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - record and express using text, pictures, charts and video — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - narrate clearly and completely — [CL 3-6 · HCL 3-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - describe so content is concrete and substantial — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6]
   - use language accumulated from life and reading — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]
5. **Revise one's work**
   - check and correct wording using reference tools and resources — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6]
   - conduct self- and peer-assessment under the teacher's guidance — [CL 5-6 · **HCL 4-6**]

Objectives 3 and 5 carry no 基础华文 span at all: FCL writing is complete sentences, correct punctuation and clear narration, not composition planning or revision. **组织成篇 — structuring a whole piece — begins at P4 in both CL and HCL**, which sets where 写话/写段 ends and a full composition starts.

## 书面互动 Written interaction

*Material types:* 贺卡, 便条, 书信, 短信, 电邮, 网络贴文. *Methods:* respect others' views and feelings; communicate responsibly (in conveying information and in voicing opinions). *Attitudes:* willing to share and exchange with others in written Chinese.

*Knowledge and skills (p32):*

1. **Use appropriate means and language**
   - choose a medium that fits the purpose (a card to convey feeling, a note to exchange information, a forum post to exchange views) — [CL 1-6 · HCL 1-6 · FCL 5-6]
   - master the **format and conventions** of each medium (card, note, letter, email) — [CL 2-6 · HCL 2-6 · FCL 5-6]
2. **Interact purposefully in writing**
   - raise questions and respond appropriately in writing — [CL 3-6 · HCL 3-6]
   - express views and respond to others' — [CL 4-6 · HCL 4-6]
   - choose content and wording to suit situation and audience — [CL 5-6 · HCL 5-6]

基础华文 carries objective 1 only. An FCL written-interaction task is "write a note / a card in the right format", not "reply to this post with your view".

# Character targets 识字量与写字量

The syllabus sets a band per course per stage, cumulative — the figure is the total reached by the end of those two years, not an addition to it (p28, p30):

| By end of | 华文 识读 | 高级华文 识读 | 基础华文 识读 | 华文 识写 | 高级华文 识写 | 基础华文 识写 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P2** | 700-750 | 700-750 | — | 300-350 | 400-450 | — |
| **P4** | 1300-1350 | 1300-1350 | — | 700-750 | 950-1000 | — |
| **P6** | 1600-1700 | 1800-1900 | 1300-1350 | 1000-1100 | 1400-1500 | 700-750 |

Read across: **华文 and 高级华文 require the same recognition load until the end of P4** and differ only in writing, and **基础华文 at the end of P6 is asked for what 华文 reached at the end of P4** — the whole of FCL's two years sits inside material a CL child has already met. That is the single most useful fact for pitching FCL work: draw on 华文 P1-P4 characters, not on P5-P6 ones.

The character lists themselves validate against the end-of-P6 bands:

| Course | Recognised by end P6 | Written by end P6 | Syllabus band |
|---|---|---|---|
| 华文 | 1655 | 1076 | 1600-1700 ✓ / 1000-1100 ✓ |
| 高级华文 | 1815 | 1425 | 1800-1900 ✓ / 1400-1500 ✓ |

Three things to read from this table:

- **识写字 is always a subset of 识读字.** A child recognises a character before being asked to write it, and many characters are read for a year or more before writing is required. Verified: every character in a write list appears in some read list.
- **华文 and 高级华文 share an identical recognition list through P4** (1219 characters at 四下). They diverge only from 五上. Through the foundation-building stage, HCL differs by asking for *more writing* of the same characters — 960 vs 736 by end of P4 — not by reading more.
- **At the mid-stage checkpoints the lists and the bands do not coincide.** The end-of-P6 totals land inside the bands, but at end of P2 the recognition lists run under band (630 against 700-750 for both courses) and the 华文 write list runs over it (368 against 300-350), and at end of P4 recognition is again under band (1219 against 1300-1350). The bands are the 2024 standard's requirement; the lists are what the 《欢乐伙伴》 series actually teaches. Where they disagree, quote whichever one the question is about, and say which it is.

## The character data file

[`chinese-characters.json`](chinese-characters.json) holds the actual characters, per semester, per course:

```
{ "华文": { "一上": { "识读字": "衣一五雨我牙鱼...", "识写字": "一五木八大..." }, ... },
  "高级华文": { ... } }
```

Use it to:

- **Check a character is in-syllabus** before putting it in a worksheet, and at which level it is first *read* versus first *written*.
- **Write 听写 (dictation) and 默写 lists** that draw only on 识写字 up to the child's semester.

Keys are semesters (`一上` = P1 first half … `六下` = P6 second half). 基础华文 has no separate list; it is P5-P6 only, and its end-of-P6 bands (1300-1350 read, 700-750 written) are the 华文 end-of-P4 bands — so pitch FCL character work from the 华文 columns up to 四下 (1219 read, 736 written), not from the P5-P6 rows.

# Humanities 人文素养

Applies to P1-P6 华文 and 高级华文, and P5-P6 基础华文.

- **价值观 values** — know oneself and respect others; care for family and honour elders; care for society and love the nation; cherish nature and care for the world; be resolute and enterprising; be earnest and responsible; distinguish right from wrong and have a sense of justice.
- **华族文化 Chinese culture** — traditional culture and arts; traditional festivals and customs; notable historical figures and stories; cultural elements within the language and script.
- **新加坡本土文化 local Singaporean culture** — local things and customs; stories of Singapore's pioneers and its history; Singapore's multiracial and multicultural society.
- **跨文化 cross-cultural** — a broad outlook and worldview; other countries' cultures and customs; notable figures and their stories; multiple perspectives and tolerance for a diverse society.

Cultural content is examinable material and a source of composition topics — festivals, family relationships, local heritage, pioneer stories.

# General competencies 通用能力

- **思维能力 thinking** — observation, imagination and creativity; posing and analysing problems and exploring solutions; connecting knowledge to real life and applying it flexibly; adaptability in adjusting goals, plans and methods; simple analysis, comparison and evaluation.
- **学习能力 learning** — self-directed and collaborative learning; metacognitive strategies to plan, evaluate, reflect on and adjust learning; appropriate learning methods, tools and resources; emotional-regulation strategies to sustain a positive attitude.
- **社交与情绪管理能力 social and emotional** — know one's strengths, needs and values; manage emotions and handle stress; respect difference and take others' perspectives; communicate, cooperate and build relationships; make responsible decisions, weighing circumstances and ethics.
- **资讯科技运用能力 ICT** — communicate using ICT; self-directed and online learning; gather and process information from the internet and digital resources; use common software (word processing, presentations) to organise material and present reports.

# Teaching and assessment

**Teaching principles** — teach less, learn more (少教多学): handle the teaching-learning relationship so teaching promotes learning rather than replacing it. Differentiate for ability and build confidence, valuing effort and making explicit teaching available to those who need it. Strengthen language practice: provide abundant meaningful, interesting, comprehensible input and build a natural language environment with task-based learning. Connect to real-life use, drawing on home and community bilingual resources. The teaching flow is **引—学—练—用—评** (lead in, learn, practise, apply, assess), spiralling upward.

**Assessment principles** —

- Plan assessment against teaching objectives, with specific and clear focal points; keep the number and weighting of tests appropriate so they do not burden students.
- **Formative assessment** carries the emphasis — effective questioning, regular and timely feedback, peer and self-assessment woven into daily teaching. Summative assessment builds on it. Feedback should be positive, specific and targeted, prompting self-reflection.
- Assess comprehensively: two or more language skills may be assessed together, and assessment should span cognitive levels including higher-order thinking — analysis, problem solving, critical and creative thinking.
- Use varied means: simulate real situations and authentic problems; use self-assessment, peer assessment, joint teacher-student assessment, assignments, learning portfolios and checklists.

**Paper format and weightings** — 《小学华文课程标准2024》 **states none**. It is a curriculum standard, not an examination syllabus: it fixes what is taught and at which grade, says the PSLE Chinese paper is pitched to the 核心单元, and on quantity says only that the number and weighting of tests must be appropriate and must not burden students. It names no components, no durations and no mark splits. So when a user asks for a "full P5 华文 paper", build it from the objective spans above and from their own school's format if they have one, and say plainly that the component weightings are not in the syllabus rather than inventing a split that will read as authoritative. The parts that *are* sourced: pitch to 核心单元; assess two or more language skills together where it fits; span the cognitive levels including analysis, problem solving, and critical and creative thinking.

**Material selection** — texts should be sound in both content and language, current, and reflect local character. Lower primary may use 韵文 (verse); middle and upper primary are mainly 记叙文 (narrative) and 实用文 (functional/practical writing). Material types include 童话, 寓言, 儿歌, 故事, on everyday life and general topics.

**Implementation years** — 华文 and 高级华文 by level: P1 2024, P2 2025, P3 2026, P4 2027, P5 2028, P6 2029. 基础华文: P5 2028, P6 2029.

# Marking conventions

- **汉字 accuracy** — a character is right or wrong; 笔画 errors (missing or added strokes), wrong 偏旁/部首, and 错别字 (wrong character, including homophone substitutions) all count as errors. 间架结构 and legibility are assessed in handwriting.
- **汉语拼音** — tone marks are part of the answer. 声母, 韵母 and 声调 each carry accuracy.
- **Writing 写作** — assess content (内容), language (语言) and organisation (结构) together. At lower levels the expectation is 写话 then 写段; a full composition (成篇) belongs to the later foundation-building stage onward.
- **标点符号** — correct punctuation is an explicit objective; Chinese punctuation conventions apply (。，、；：""《》！？).
- Pitch any practice paper to **核心单元** level unless the user asks for 深广单元 enrichment.
