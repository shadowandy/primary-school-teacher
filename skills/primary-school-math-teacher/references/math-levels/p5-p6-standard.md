# Mathematics — Primary 5 and 6 (Standard)

Quoted from MOE *Mathematics Syllabus Primary One to Six*, 2021 Primary 1 cohort, updated Oct 2025, which is not shipped with the skill; the `(pNN)` markers record which of its pages a claim came from rather than pointing anywhere you need to go. How to teach it, the marking conventions, the calculator tags, paper-setting, the Standard-vs-Foundation summary and the progression chains are in [`../math.md`](../math.md).

Standard continues the P1-P4 content for the majority of students. The Foundation levels are in [`p5-p6-foundation.md`](p5-p6-foundation.md).

## Primary 5 (Standard)

**Number and Algebra** — *Whole Numbers, 1. Numbers up to 10 million:* reading and writing numbers in numerals and in words — that single outcome is the whole topic. *2. Four Operations:* multiplying and dividing by 10, 100, 1000 and their multiples without calculator; order of operations without calculator; use of brackets without calculator. *Fractions, 1. Fraction and Division:* dividing a whole number by a whole number with quotient as a fraction; expressing fractions as decimals. *2. Four Operations:* adding and subtracting mixed numbers; multiplying a proper/improper fraction and a whole number without calculator; multiplying a proper fraction and a proper/improper fraction without calculator; multiplying two improper fractions; multiplying a mixed number and a whole number. *Decimals, 1. Four Operations:* multiplying and dividing decimals (up to 3 decimal places) by 10, 100, 1000 and their multiples without calculator; converting a measurement from a smaller unit to a larger unit in decimal form and vice versa, for km and m, m and cm, kg and g, ℓ and mℓ. *Percentage, 1. Percentage:* expressing a part of a whole as a percentage; use of %; finding a percentage part of a whole; finding discount, GST and annual interest. *Rate, 1. Rate:* rate as the amount of a quantity per unit of another quantity; finding rate, total amount or number of units given the other two quantities. (p41)

**Measurement and Geometry** — *Area and Volume, 1. Area of Triangle:* concepts of base and height of a triangle; area of triangle; finding the area of composite figures made up of rectangles, squares and triangles. *2. Volume of Cube and Cuboid:* building solids with unit cubes; measuring volume in cubic units, cm³/m³, "excluding conversion between cm³ and m³"; drawing cubes and cuboids on isometric grid; volume of a cube/cuboid; finding the volume of liquid in a rectangular tank; relationship between ℓ (or mℓ) with cm³. *Geometry, 1. Angles:* angles on a straight line; angles at a point; vertically opposite angles; finding unknown angles. *2. Triangle:* properties of isosceles triangle, equilateral triangle, right-angled triangle; angle sum of a triangle; finding unknown angles "without additional construction of lines". *3. Parallelogram, Rhombus and Trapezium:* properties of parallelogram, rhombus and trapezium; finding unknown angles "without additional construction of lines". (p42)

**Statistics** — no Statistics sub-strand is listed at Standard P5. Data handling resumes at P6 with Average; the P4 graph and table work stands as prior learning.

Boundaries at P5 Standard:

- Volume: "measuring volume in cubic units, cm³/m³, **excluding conversion between cm³ and m³**".
- Angles in triangles and in the special quadrilaterals: "finding unknown angles **without additional construction of lines**" — stated twice, once for each topic. A question that needs an auxiliary line drawn is out.
- Division of fractions is **not** at P5; it is P6. "Dividing a whole number by a whole number with quotient as a fraction" is a P5 outcome and is a different thing — dividing *by* a fraction is not available.
- Fraction multiplication is listed case by case: proper/improper × whole, proper × proper/improper, improper × improper, mixed number × whole. Mixed number × mixed number and mixed number × fraction are not listed.
- Decimal multiplication and division at P5 is "by 10, 100, 1000 and their multiples" only. Decimal × decimal is not at Standard P5 — it appears, unrestricted, at Foundation P6.
- Percentage runs one way only: part expressed as a percentage, and a percentage part of a whole, with discount, GST and annual interest as the applications. Finding the whole given a part, and percentage increase/decrease, are P6.
- No ratio and no algebra at P5 — both are Standard P6, which is why word problems here stay in bars and units.
- Whole numbers reach 10 million for reading and writing in numerals and words; nothing else is listed at that range.

## Primary 6 (Standard)

**Number and Algebra** — *Fractions, 1. Four Operations:* dividing a proper fraction by a whole number without calculator; dividing a whole number / proper fraction by a proper fraction without calculator. *Percentage, 1. Percentage:* finding the whole given a part and the percentage; finding percentage increase/decrease. *Ratio, 1. Ratio:* notation, representations and interpretation of a:b and a:b:c, "where a, b and c are whole numbers, excluding ratios involving fractions and decimals"; equivalent ratios; dividing a quantity in a given ratio; expressing a ratio in its simplest form; finding the ratio of two or three given quantities; finding the missing term in a pair of equivalent ratios; relationship between fraction and ratio. *Algebra, 1. Algebra:* using a letter to represent an unknown number; notation, representations and interpretation of simple algebraic expressions such as a ± 3, a × 3 or 3a, a ÷ 3 or a/3; "simplifying simple linear expressions excluding brackets"; evaluating simple linear expressions by substitution; "simple linear equations involving whole number coefficient only". (p43)

**Measurement and Geometry** — *Area and Volume, 1. Area and Circumference of Circle:* area and circumference of circle; finding the area and perimeter of semicircle and quarter circle; finding the area and perimeter of composite figures made up of square, rectangle, triangle, semicircle and quarter circle. *2. Volume of Cube and Cuboid:* finding one dimension of a cuboid given its volume and the other dimensions; finding the length of one edge of a cube given its volume; finding the height of a cuboid given its volume and base area; finding the area of a face of a cuboid given its volume and one dimension; use of √ and ∛. *Geometry, 1. Special Quadrilaterals:* finding unknown angles, "without additional construction of lines", in composite geometric figures involving square, rectangle, triangle, parallelogram, rhombus and trapezium. (p44)

**Statistics** — *Data Analysis, 1. Average of a Set of Data:* average as 'total value ÷ number of data'; relationship between average, total value and number of data. (p44)

Boundaries at P6 Standard:

- Ratio: "a:b and a:b:c, where a, b and c are whole numbers, **excluding ratios involving fractions and decimals**".
- Algebra: "**simplifying** simple linear expressions **excluding brackets**", and "simple linear equations involving **whole number coefficient only**". No expansion or factorisation, no two unknowns, no simultaneous equations, no negative or fractional coefficients, and no brackets to expand — so a problem-sum method that needs to cross-multiply a bracket stays in units rather than becoming algebra.
- Geometry: "finding unknown angles, **without additional construction of lines**".
- Circles: area and circumference of the circle, and the semicircle and quarter circle. No other sector or arc, no angle-at-centre work. Composite figures are made of "square, rectangle, triangle, semicircle and quarter circle" only. The syllabus does not state which value of π to use — take it from the child's own paper, which normally prints it.
- Average is the mean only, as 'total value ÷ number of data'. Median, mode and range are not in the primary syllabus.
- Fraction division is the two listed cases: proper fraction ÷ whole number, and whole number or proper fraction ÷ proper fraction. Dividing *by* a mixed or improper fraction is not listed.
- P6 Standard adds no new Whole Numbers, Decimals, Measurement or Data Representation content — everything there is carried from P5 and below.
