# Mathematics — Primary 1 and 2

Quoted from MOE *Mathematics Syllabus Primary One to Six*, 2021 Primary 1 cohort, updated Oct 2025, which is not shipped with the skill; the `(pNN)` markers record which of its pages a claim came from rather than pointing anywhere you need to go. How to teach it, the marking conventions, the calculator tags, paper-setting, the Standard-vs-Foundation summary and the progression chains are in [`../math.md`](../math.md).

Both levels are common to all students; the Standard / Foundation split starts at P5.

## Primary 1

**Number and Algebra** — *Whole Numbers, 1. Numbers up to 100:* counting to tell the number of objects in a given set; number notation, representations and place values (tens, ones); reading and writing numbers in numerals and in words; comparing the number of objects in two or more sets; comparing and ordering numbers; patterns in number sequences; ordinal numbers (first, second, up to tenth) and symbols (1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.). *2. Addition and Subtraction:* concepts of addition and subtraction; use of +, − and =; relationship between addition and subtraction; adding more than two 1-digit numbers; adding and subtracting within 100; adding and subtracting using algorithms; mental calculation — within 20, of a 2-digit number and ones without renaming, and of a 2-digit number and tens. *3. Multiplication and Division:* concepts of multiplication and division; use of ×; multiplying within 40; dividing within 20. *Money, 1. Money:* counting amount of money in cents up to $1, and in dollars up to $100. (p31)

**Measurement and Geometry** — *Measurement, 1. Length:* measuring length in centimetres; use of abbreviation cm; comparing and ordering lengths in cm; measuring and drawing a line segment to the nearest cm. *2. Time:* telling time to 5 minutes; use of 'am' and 'pm'; use of abbreviations h and min; duration of one hour / half hour. *Geometry, 1. 2D Shapes:* identifying, naming, describing and classifying rectangle, square, triangle, circle, half circle, quarter circle; forming different 2D figures with rectangle, square, triangle, half circle and quarter circle; identifying the 2D shapes that make up a given figure; copying figures on dot grid or square grid. (p32)

**Statistics** — *Data Representation and Interpretation, 1. Picture Graphs:* reading and interpreting data from picture graphs. (p32)

Boundaries at P1:

- Whole numbers stop at 100. Multiplication is "multiplying within 40", division "dividing within 20".
- Mental calculation is only the three listed cases — within 20, a 2-digit number and ones "without renaming", a 2-digit number and tens.
- The ÷ symbol is not introduced at P1 (use of ÷ is a P2 outcome), so division here is grouping and sharing, spoken and drawn rather than written as a ÷ sentence.
- The circle is named and classified, but the shapes used for "forming different 2D figures" are rectangle, square, triangle, half circle and quarter circle — the whole circle is not one of them.
- Time is told to 5 minutes only; telling time to the minute is P2.
- Length is measured in cm only; m, kg/g and ℓ are P2, and km and mℓ are P3.
- No fractions, no decimals at P1.

## Primary 2

**Number and Algebra** — *Whole Numbers, 1. Numbers up to 1000:* counting in tens/hundreds; number notation, representations and place values (hundreds, tens, ones); numerals and words; comparing and ordering numbers; patterns in number sequences; odd and even numbers. *2. Addition and Subtraction:* addition and subtraction algorithms (up to 3 digits); mental calculation of a 3-digit number and ones/tens/hundreds. *3. Multiplication and Division:* multiplication tables of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10; use of ÷; relationship between multiplication and division; multiplying and dividing within the multiplication tables; mental calculation within the tables of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10. *Fractions, 1. Fraction of a Whole:* fraction as part of a whole; notation and representations of fractions; comparing and ordering unit fractions and like fractions, "with denominators of given fractions not exceeding 12". *2. Addition and Subtraction:* adding and subtracting like fractions within one whole, denominators not exceeding 12. *Money, 1. Money:* counting amount of money in dollars and cents; reading and writing money in decimal notation; comparing two or three amounts of money; converting an amount of money in decimal notation to cents only, and vice versa. (p33)

**Measurement and Geometry** — *Measurement, 1. Length, Mass and Volume:* measuring length in metres, mass in kilograms/grams, volume of liquid in litres; using appropriate units of measurement and their abbreviations m, g, kg, ℓ; comparing and ordering lengths, masses and volumes. *2. Time:* telling time to the minute; measuring time in hours and minutes; converting time in hours and minutes to minutes only, and vice versa. *Geometry, 1. 2D Shapes:* making/completing patterns with 2D shapes according to one or two of size, shape, colour, orientation. *2. 3D Shapes:* identifying, naming, describing and classifying cube, cuboid, cone, cylinder, sphere. (p34)

**Statistics** — *Data Representation and Interpretation, 1. Picture Graphs with Scales:* reading and interpreting data from picture graphs with scales. (p34)

Boundaries at P2:

- Fractions are compared and ordered as "unit fractions" and "like fractions" only, "with denominators of given fractions not exceeding 12". Unlike denominators come at P3.
- Fraction addition and subtraction is *like* fractions "within one whole" — same denominator, and no answer above 1. Related and unrelated denominators are P3 and P4.
- No equivalent fractions and no simplest form at P2.
- Multiplication tables are 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10. Tables 6-9, division with remainder, and multiplying or dividing beyond the tables are P3.
- Algorithms reach 3 digits; numbers reach 1000.
- 2D shape patterns vary "one or two" attributes, no more.
- 3D shapes are identified, named, described and classified only — nets, 2D representations and volume come later.
- Decimal notation appears only as money; decimals as a topic are P4.
