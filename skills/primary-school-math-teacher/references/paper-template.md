# Paper template — practice and exam papers

The house format for any practice set or paper this skill writes, printable as it stands and markable by a parent.

It is a **formatting convention, not MOE's**: the syllabus prescribes no paper format, no question counts, no mark totals and no duration. The child's own school paper is the only authority on the structure in front of them — follow theirs where it differs, and keep this shape only for what it does not settle. `math.md` stays the authority on what a level covers and on the marking conventions the key applies.

## How to fill it

- **Header first.** Level, stream and the topics covered go in the title; total marks go in the header, and they are the sum of the parts.
- **Mark every question** in square brackets at the end of the stem — `[2m]` for a part, `[5 Marks]` for a multi-part question's total.
- **Keep the sections the child sits.** Section A multiple-choice with four numbered options and bracketed answer space; Section B short answer and structured word problems worth 2-5 marks each. Drop a section the child's paper does not have, and rename it to match a paper that names it differently.
- **Leave real working space.** A word problem gets 4-6 blank lines, or a labelled space for the bar model where the method calls for one.
- **Singapore units and contexts throughout** — `$`, cents, kg, g, km, m, mℓ, ℓ, hawker centres, MRT, Sentosa. Money in decimal notation from P2 (`$4.05`).
- **Answer key last**, as Part 3, always — never beside the question.

## The key is the check

Write the key in the method the child is taught — bars included — with a mark for each step, and then solve every question from the key again against the level's range limits. A question whose own answer needs an out-of-syllabus step is the most common failure in paper-setting, and reaching for algebra in the key is how it hides.
