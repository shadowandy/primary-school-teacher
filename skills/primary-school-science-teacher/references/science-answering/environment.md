## Living together, food chains and food webs

- A **population** is the organisms of one species in a community; three populations of insects means three species — grasshoppers, butterflies, bees — and a population size counts young and adults alike, larvae and pupae included. A single organism is not a population.
- **Producer in a food web** — *Organism C does not depend on any other organism for food. It is a producer and makes its own food* — read it off the arrows.

## Adaptation

Adaptations are features or behaviours that let a living thing survive where it lives: **structural** (a physical feature performing a function — thick fur, pitcher leaves, talons) or **behavioural** (an action — an alligator lying in wait, a millipede curling up).

Each answer ends at survival, through the mechanism:

- **Spread-out leaves** — less overlapping, larger surface area to absorb more sunlight, faster photosynthesis, more food.
- **Needle-like leaves** — smaller leaf surface area, less water vapour lost, so the plant survives where water is scarce.
- **Camouflage** — body colouring, pattern or parts blend with the surroundings, so predators cannot spot the animal **easily** — never "cannot spot it". Predators camouflage to ambush prey too.
- **Mimicry** — the animal resembles a poisonous or unpleasant-tasting animal, so predators may mistake it for that animal and are less likely to feed on it.
- **Cold places** — a thick layer of fur or feathers traps air; air is a poor conductor of heat and conducts heat from the animal to the surroundings at a slower rate, so the animal retains more heat.
- **Hot places** — less contact surface area with the hot ground means heat is gained from it at a slower rate: *standing on two feet gives the lizard less contact surface area with the hot sand, so it gains heat from the sand at a slower rate*, and alternating feet lets the raised pair *lose heat to the cooler surrounding air* — both preventing heat injuries. A larger exposed surface area works the other way: *the elephant's large ears increase the exposed surface area of its body, giving a greater area over which to lose heat to the cooler surroundings*. The endpoint of a hot-place adaptation is avoiding heat injury, not simply "staying cool".

## Man's impact on the environment

Cause and effect, kept in the right order:

| Cause | Effect |
|---|---|
| Deforestation | Soil erosion; higher surrounding temperatures; fewer kinds of plants and animals; global warming |
| Global warming | Ice caps melting faster; extreme weather |
| Burning of fossil fuels, air pollution | Acid rain |
| Burning of fossil fuels, forest fires, burning of crops | Haze; respiratory illness |
| Water pollution, oil spills | Falling populations of marine animals and sea birds |

- **Acid rain** — *Burning fossil fuels produces air pollutants. The pollutants rise and mix with the water in the clouds. The rainwater then falls as acid rain*. Deforestation does not cause acid rain: cutting trees releases no pollutant gases.
- "More trees are cut down" is not an effect of deforestation — it is deforestation. Restating the cause as its own effect is a standard distractor.
