# Science frames — light, electricity, forces and energy

Themes: Energy (light, energy conversion), Systems (electrical system), Interactions (forces).

The five moves every answer makes, the boundary notes and the pitch are in [`../science-answering-techniques.md`](../science-answering-techniques.md); the misconceptions to name when marking are in [`misconceptions.md`](misconceptions.md).

## Light and shadows

- **Shadow** — *Light from the lamp travels to the box. The box blocks some of the light. A shadow is formed on the surface behind the box, extending in the same direction as the light travels*.
- **Seeing a non-luminous object** — *Light from the lamp shines on the cat. The cat reflects the light into Shafiq's eyes, enabling him to see the cat*.

## Electricity

- **Series** — one path for electricity to flow. A fused bulb makes it an open circuit, no electricity flows, and no bulb lights.
- **Parallel** — more than one path. A fused bulb leaves the other bulbs in their own closed circuits, so they still light.
- **Bulb connection** — one end of the wire to the **metal casing**, the other to the **metal tip**, and the bulb lights. Both wires on the casing means no current through the filament and that bulb stays dark — while the casing still conducts, so the loop is closed and the rest of the circuit works. Check each component, not only the whole loop. Such a question is answered bulb by bulb: *Bulb B is connected to both the tip and the casing, so electric current flows through bulb B's filament and it lights up. Bulb A has both wires connected to the casing, so no electric current flows through bulb A's filament and it does not light up*.
- **An insulator in a branch** opens that branch only: in a parallel circuit a break in one branch darkens that bulb alone. The frame is *Material L is an electrical insulator and is arranged in series with B3. The circuit connecting L and B3 is open, so no electric current flows through B3 and B3 does not light up* — the other branches keep their own closed loops and stay lit.
- **Bulbs added in series** → less current through each bulb → all dimmer. **Bulbs added in parallel** → the current through each bulb is unchanged → brightness unchanged. **Batteries added in series** → more current through each bulb → brighter. Comparing two circuits takes the comparative form: *there are more batteries in circuit C than in circuit B, so more electric current flows through B3 than through B2, and B3 shines more brightly than B2*.
- **Electromagnet** — *When the switch is closed, the circuit is closed. Electric current flows through the coils of wire around the iron rod. The iron rod is magnetised to become an electromagnet, which exerts a magnetic force of attraction on the magnetic steel paper clip*. Opening the switch stops the current, the electromagnet loses all its magnetism, and the force disappears.
- The core must be a **magnetic** material, and the current flows in the coils, not through the core. A glass core leaves the bulb lit and the nail unmoved: glass being a non-magnetic material is the reason, not glass being an electrical insulator.

## Forces

- *The compressed spring exerts an elastic spring force **on** the object* — the force is exerted on something, and a spring at its original length exerts none.
- *The magnet exerts a magnetic force of attraction on the magnetic material* — rather than "the magnet pulls it".
- *There is frictional force **between** the wooden block and the sandpaper*, hence the block slows down. Friction is a contact force, so both surfaces are named.

## Energy

- **Conversion** — *the __form__ energy of __object__ is converted to the __form__ energy of __object__*: each form tied to the object holding it, source form first. *The potential energy of the battery is converted to the electrical energy of the circuit*.
- **More or less of it** — state the change in the condition, then carry *more* or *less* through every stage: *released from a greater height → more potential energy → more kinetic energy as it falls → more sound energy when it lands → a louder sound*.
- **Useful energy** — name the conversion the device is for; heat is usually the waste, not the answer. A computer playing a video: *the electrical energy of the circuit is converted to the light and sound energy of the computer*.

A question that says *use energy conversion* is answered with energy, not with force. The worked case is a catapult shooting a pebble further when the rubber is pulled back further: *when the rubber is pulled back further it stores more potential energy, which is converted to more kinetic energy of the moving pebble when it is released.* **Result:** *the pebble travels further*. Both ends of the conversion are needed — naming only the kinetic energy the pebble gains leaves out where it came from, and "more force was used" answers a different question.
