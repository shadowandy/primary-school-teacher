# Science — concept notes by theme

Everything needed to teach from these notes — every definition, classification table, experiment set-up and everyday application — is written out here or in the theme file this one routes to. Nothing here depends on any external document.

**This is not MOE syllabus.** It is classroom revision notes: the key ideas, definitions, examples, classifications, experiments and everyday applications of each topic, as commonly taught for PSLE. It carries no authority over what a level covers, and `science.md` stays the authority on content and on every boundary. Apply it, but do not quote it as MOE.

These notes are for **what the science is** — explaining a topic, building a revision sheet, choosing examples and set-ups for a question. **How an open-ended answer is written and marked** lives in [`science-answering-techniques.md`](science-answering-techniques.md) and its topic files; each theme file below points there rather than restating a frame.

## How the theme files are written

- **Levels come from `science.md`**, never from the notes' own ordering. Every `##` topic carries its level and stream, e.g. `## Elastic spring force — P6 Standard only`. When the two disagree, `science.md` wins.
- **Boundaries are applied in place.** Where classroom notes commonly use a term `science.md` records as *not required* — *translucent*, *phloem*, *stomata*, *elastic potential energy*, *nickel and cobalt* — the file either uses the in-boundary phrasing or flags the term inline with what to say instead. Never lift a flagged term into a question or model answer at that level.
- **Reaching ahead is labelled.** A P3 note that touches a later topic (fungi "cannot make their own food") says which level explains it, and is stated as fact rather than explained at the earlier level.
- **`## Beyond the current syllabus`** closes most files. It holds classroom content with no outcome in the current syllabus — cell parts, what makes an electromagnet stronger, colour and heat absorption, what kinetic energy depends on. It is background for an adult or a curious child; never set or mark it.
- **Classroom figures are not outcomes.** Percentages, rates and named species (air composition, resting heart rate, *Lactobacillus*) are illustrations; do not test recall of them.
